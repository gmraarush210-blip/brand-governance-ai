# Research and Validation Plan

**Evidence status:** no primary research collected yet. The problem statement is the initial evidence;
frequency and cost of the problem are hypotheses until measured.

| Method | What we do | Decision informed |
|---|---|---|
| Interviews (5+) | Creators, brand/comms managers, reviewers: their last real review, tools, delays, recurring corrections | Prioritise checks; define first workflow |
| Short survey | Role, content volume, error types, current review effort | Test the pain; set pilot baseline |
| Scenario test | Users submit a draft, inspect cited findings, revise, route for approval; record completion and confusion | Improve explanations and workflow |

Pilot measures: review completion, traceable resolution, repeated-issue rate, user-reported usefulness.
